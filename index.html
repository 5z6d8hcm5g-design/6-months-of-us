Six months later.

Six months is made up of so many little things.

It's the first conversation that started all of this. It's “can we lock in?” It's the first “I love you.” It's a video call. It's a hug I still remember because it felt safe. It's three bracelets. It's the drawing I made for you. It's the “100 things I love about you” list. It's silly TikToks, Roblox, Bitmojis, songs, inside jokes and all the ordinary moments that became important because they were ours.

I hope you know how deeply I love you. I hope you know how much I value what we've built and how seriously I take the place you've earned in my life.

I want you to feel safe with me. I want you to know that you are loved by me. I want to show you that love, not just tell you about it.

I don't know exactly what every future chapter will look like, but I know I'm grateful this chapter exists. And I'm grateful that it has you in it.

Happy six months, my sweet boy. 💜
